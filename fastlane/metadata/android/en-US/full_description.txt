ScootPit BMS is an Android dashboard and ride log app for the Joyor T6E Pro with a compatible JBD/Jiabaida Bluetooth battery management system (BMS). It was developed with the Joyor T6E Pro and JBD SP14S004 and automatically records scooter rides while showing battery and ride data live.

<ul>
<li>Connects to a configured BMS and records rides when the configured riding conditions are met</li>
<li>Detects slow GPS movement without motor load as walking, excludes it from scooter distance, and pauses the ride after an adjustable delay</li>
<li>Tracks GPS route, distance, moving and stopped time, and trip statistics</li>
<li>Shows battery level, voltage, current, power, energy use, estimated range, and temperatures</li>
<li>Customizes the dashboard with gauges, bars, number tiles, photos, and text; gauges rotate while readings stay horizontal</li>
<li>Starts with an airy portrait layout and a compact landscape cockpit with readiness status in a shorter header</li>
<li>Colors scales with custom start, middle, and end colors and adjustable transition thresholds</li>
<li>Reverses bars and gauge scales per tile without changing the reading</li>
<li>Sets the live consumption distance per scooter from 100 to 1000 m with a slider</li>
<li>Imports single GPX or CSV rides and restores complete ride journals from folders</li>
<li>Skips rides already in the journal; an “All” view shows the complete date range</li>
</ul>

Requires Android 8 or later and a compatible JBD/Jiabaida Bluetooth BMS. The app was developed with the Joyor T6e Pro and JBD SP14S004; compatibility with other BMS models is not guaranteed. Bluetooth and location permissions are used for device scanning and GPS ride recording. The app interface is currently in German.

Privacy: Profiles and ride files are stored locally. Optional weather updates send rounded location coordinates to Open-Meteo. Opening a map loads the visible map tiles from OpenStreetMap.
